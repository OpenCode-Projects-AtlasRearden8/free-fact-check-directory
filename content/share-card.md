# The Group-Chat Share Card

A short card you can copy and paste into a family or group chat when
forwarded posts keep needing corrections. The plain-text version
works in any chat app, SMS, email, or forum. Share it with kindness —
nobody likes being told they were fooled.

## Plain-text card (copy everything between the lines)

---

**PAUSE BEFORE YOU SHARE: the 2-minute check**

1. **Who profits?** Follow the link in the bio or post.
2. **Name + date?** Claims without a source or a date stay unshared.
3. **Image check:** upload the photo at images.google.com — is it
   older than the event it claims to be from?
4. **Official site:** search cdc.gov, irs.gov, weather.gov, or
   census.gov yourself for the "big news" in the post.
5. **Still unsure?** Search the first sentence in quotes at
   snopes.com or politifact.com.

If it passes 1 to 4, share away. If not, silence is kindness too.

*This card is free from the Free Fact-Check Directory.*

---

## The rule in one sentence

Nowhere-forwards is not the goal — checked-forwards are the goal:
a post that survives two minutes of checking is worth sharing, and
a post that fails is worth replying to with this card.

## Tips for sharing it

- Post it once at a calm moment, not in the middle of an argument.
- Use it as a reply to yourself, so family members see it without
  being singled out.
- Save it in your phone's notes app so it is ready to paste.
- Change nothing in the links; they lead straight to free tools.

## Why framing matters, in one breath

A forwarded post is not a library card — it is a message written by
someone who wants something. The card simply asks that message to
show its name, its date, and its receipts before it gets room to
travel further.
